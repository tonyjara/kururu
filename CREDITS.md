# Credits

Kururu's mascots are other people's pixel art. This is whose, and under what
terms. The app shows the same list in **Settings → About → Credits**, from
`web/src/credits.ts`, and `web/test/credits.test.ts` keeps the two in step.

- The sheets in [`assets/mascots/`](assets/mascots) each carry their own
  `LICENCE.txt`, with the source, the licence and what was changed to fit the
  sheet into kururu's grid.
- The earlier mascots were installed from `../kururu-styles/mascots/<id>/`,
  where each has its `LICENCE.txt` beside its sheet.
- The interface icons are Lucide's, ISC: [`web/src/icons.LICENSE`](web/src/icons.LICENSE).
