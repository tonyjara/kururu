WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is one 8-frame GIF per character (28x30). Its frames are laid along row 0. Working and idle are the same loop, idle slower.

Every frame was then cropped by one box, the union of every frame on the sheet,
and set in a square cell of that box's longer side, standing on the cell's floor
and centred across it, so the trim is the whole cell and nothing is clipped. No
pixel was redrawn or rescaled.
