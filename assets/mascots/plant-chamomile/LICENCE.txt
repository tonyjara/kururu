WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is one RPG Maker charset (plant_enemies_charset.png): eight characters, each three walk frames by four facings at 48x48. This sheet is one character's row facing the viewer: row 0 the standing frame, row 1 the walk as stand, right step, stand, left step.

Every frame was then cropped by one box, the union of every frame on the sheet,
and set in a square cell of that box's longer side, standing on the cell's floor
and centred across it, so the trim is the whole cell and nothing is clipped. No
pixel was redrawn or rescaled.
